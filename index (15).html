Configuration Boundary

Components documents the physical hardware and its role in the aircraft. Firmware settings, parameter values, motor/servo functions, serial-port configuration, RC mapping, failsafes, and tuning values should be maintained under ArduPilot.

Items such as propeller models, exact battery manufacturer, connector inventory, component weights, and any hardware changes can be added as those details are verified.

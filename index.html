RC Aircraft Builds

A public engineering archive for RC aircraft projects from multiple manufacturers. Documentation is organized by manufacturer, aircraft model or family, version, and individual physical build so manufacturing records, hardware configuration, flight testing, media, and engineering analysis remain traceable to the exact aircraft.

Documentation Organization

A consistent hierarchy for every aircraft
1

Manufacturer

Flightory, Titan Dynamics, Eclipson, or another manufacturer.

2

Aircraft Model

The aircraft family or model, such as Stallion.

3

Version

Design revision or generation, such as Stallion V1 or V2.

4

Physical Build

Build 01, Build 02, etc., each with its own technical history.

Per-Build Engineering Record

🖨️ Manufacturing

3D printing, materials, slicer settings, component weights, print time, reprints, and manufacturing findings.

🔧 Assembly & Hardware

Components, wiring, connectors, installation, modifications, photos, and repair history.

🛩️ Configuration

Flight controller, firmware, ArduPilot parameters, calibration, mappings, and configuration baselines.

📊 Flight Testing

Ground tests, hover tests, flight-test objectives, observations, results, and embedded YouTube video.

📈 Engineering Analysis

BIN logs, tuning analysis, configuration comparisons, engineering assessments, and reports.

📁 Media & Downloads

Images, diagrams, PARAM files, reports, reference material, and links to externally hosted video.

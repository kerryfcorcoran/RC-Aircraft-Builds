Current Process Baseline

Primary slicer: OrcaSlicer v2.4.2 ↗

LW-PLA aerodynamic parts: primarily OrcaSlicer with 1 wall and low-density Gyroid or Lateral Lattice infill.
Structural PETG: generally 3 walls with 20% Grid or Cubic depending on part.
Thin-wall boom parts: OrcaSlicer works correctly when Detect Thin Walls is enabled and Wall Generator = Arachne.
Transparent LED covers: Detect Thin Walls enabled.

OrcaSlicer v2.4.2 ↗ — Thin-Wall Configuration

BOOM TAIL INSERT, BOOM FUS INSERT, and BOOM FUS MOUNT do not require Cura. They can be sliced successfully in OrcaSlicer with:

  • Detect Thin Walls: Enabled
  • Wall Generator: Arachne

This supersedes the earlier interim note that Cura was required.
